The Meaning of Given

A name that carries a gift.

Given comes from a simple, personal belief — that the founder herself was a gift given by God to her parents. That gratitude became a philosophy: that beauty is a gift to be received, celebrated, and shared.

Not something to be earned or bought. Something to be received — and generously given to every woman, at every budget.

Quality & Honesty

Quality you can feel.
Honesty you can trust.

Affordable should never mean careless. Every piece is chosen for the way it looks and feels in real light — softly natural, easy to wear, and made with intentionality.

  • Natural-looking appearance
  • Realistic lustre
  • Soft texture
  • Careful wig-cap construction

These are the priorities we design for — not certified guarantees. We are honest about our materials: high-grade fibre and blended hair, not human hair.
